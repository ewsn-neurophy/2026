REGISTRATION INFORMATION

At least one author per accepted paper must register for the conference and present their paper at the workshop. Notice that a full registration is not required if the paper will be presented by a student.

All attendees must also register at full or student rate depending on their eligibility even if they are not presenter.

The conference registration is valid for the whole event.

Please leave blank the article number, as workshop papers are not considered full papers.
